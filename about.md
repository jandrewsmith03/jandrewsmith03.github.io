My academic background begins at [Texas A&amp;M University](http://www.tamu.edu/). In 2017, I graduated with degrees in Economics and  Applied Mathematics.
<br/>
<br/>
Formerly, I worked as a research assistant for the [Economics Research Lab](http://erl.tamu.edu/), an experimental economics laboratory. I also served the [Council of Economics Advisers](https://www.whitehouse.gov/cea) under the Obama Administration as an intern for the Macroeconomics Group. I also have experience researching Labor Economics topics. Specifically, I studied the effect of minimum wage changes on job skill requirements with [Dr. Jonathan Meer](http://people.tamu.edu/~jmeer/).
